Welcome To The Anime Society of LCCC

We're a student organization at Lorain County Community College for anyone who enjoys anime and wants to learn more about the culture behind it.

We meet twice a week to watch shows and films, hold workshops, and spend time with people who share the interest. You don't need any background in anime to take part. Longtime fans and complete newcomers are equally welcome.

Wednesdays and Fridays, 6:00 to 8:00 PM Room UC208A, Lorain County Community College

What We Do

Screenings

Anime series and films, along with other Asian cinema and the occasional Western pick.

Workshops

Member-led presentations on the art, history, and culture of anime, open to the whole LCCC community.

Social Nights

Relaxed evenings for conversation, Japanese music, and getting to know other members.

Discord

Where we vote on what to watch next and share schedule updates between meetings.

    How The Wheel Works

    1. We spin the wheel three times and write the results on the board. Trailers are shown if anyone wants to see them.
    2. Everyone gets two votes. The two anime with the most votes are watched that night, highest vote first.
    3. After the break, a member's name is drawn at random. That member picks what we watch next and whether it's dubbed or subbed.
    4. The wheel keeps track of each show's episode number, so we pick up where we left off the next time it lands.

    Summer

    Summer works differently. We meet on Fridays only, and instead of the wheel, members vote in a lineup for the whole season on Voting Night: one shorter series (11 to 13 episodes) and one longer series (23 to 26 episodes). Each week we watch one episode of the short series and two of the long one.

    Club Rules

    The basics are below. The full rules, including warnings, appeals, and complaints, are in our Constitution (PDF).

    Conduct

    • Treat everyone with dignity and respect.
    • Keep disruptions to a minimum during screenings and activities.
    • Keep the room clean. Clear your table and throw away your trash.
    • Follow the LCCC Campus Code of Conduct, which always takes precedence.

    Anime Suggestions

    Keep suggestions suitable for a public college setting: no excessive gore or violence, nudity, or sexual content. Anything rated above TV-14 may be reviewed by the officers and advisor first.

    High School And Early College Students

    Some anime we watch includes mature content, so you'll need a waiver signed by a parent or guardian before taking part. Ask an officer for a copy.

    Becoming A Member

    Come to any meeting. You're welcome to sit in as often as you like before joining, and there's no fee. Students and non-students can both join. When you're ready, add yourself to our roster on LCCC Connect.

    Members can take part in all club activities, events, and fundraisers, join the club Discord, and vote on what we watch and on club policies.
